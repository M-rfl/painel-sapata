## Tech Debt

### Significant
- **Per-trecho deviation at inferred stations (Phase 9, unfixed).** CTR, EQD and SCT collapse the neighbouring trechos, so trechos 1 and 4 have 0 impactos and "trecho com mais eventos" differs from the workbook. Phase 9 verification asked for a fix "before per-trecho views". It never happened, and the deviation now shows in the matrix, ranking, priority, per-trecho IQ and the PDF. The candidate fix is a larger radius or a boundary at the closest GPS approach. It would need a regenerated site JSON, refreshed fixtures and parity, and a matching change in the JS converter port.
